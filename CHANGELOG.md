# Version notes

Newest first. Each entry is short enough to paste into the add-on listing's version notes.

## 1.5.21
Wide Cards: on a narrow screen, cards stay one column wide instead of growing past the screen.

## 1.5.20
Chrome and Edge: the extension's description is shortened to fit the Chrome Web Store's limit. Nothing changes in how it works.

## 1.5.19
The 24-hour clock toggle is back in Dashboard Options, right under Tasks Panel.

## 1.5.18
Tasks Panel: its options (three-day view, list length, 24-hour clock) moved to a three-dot menu beside the plus, so Dashboard Options stays short.

## 1.5.17
Tasks Panel: finished work shows its score; a three-day view; a limit on how many tasks each list shows (all, 10 or 5, with "Show more"); and a 24-hour clock option. All are in Dashboard Options.

## 1.5.16
Now also available for Chrome and Edge. Messages about allowing Gradescope or Ed access no longer name a specific browser.

## 1.5.15
What-If: section totals (Homework, Quizzes, Exams…) update as you change scores, and the total grade is now worked out from the section grades and their weights, as Brightspace does.

## 1.5.14
What-If: blank grades now start at 0 and count toward the total, so drop rules treat them like any other score.

## 1.5.13
What-If: empty grades in a section with a drop rule are dropped first, as Brightspace does, so editing another score no longer brings them back as zeros. Editing a dropped grade no longer pushes the estimate up.

## 1.5.12
Grades: grades Brightspace marks as dropped are now dropped even when they have no score yet, so they no longer count as zeros.

## 1.5.11
What-If: grades Brightspace dropped stay dropped when you change other scores in the section, so they no longer come back as zeros and lower the estimate. Changing a dropped grade brings it back and drops the next-lowest instead.

## 1.5.10
What-If: drop rules keep working after you edit a score in a section that has grades Brightspace already dropped.

## 1.5.9
Grades: totals use Brightspace's own figures where it gives them, which picks up adjustments it doesn't show students (such as a participation score counted as full marks). Dropped grades are read from Brightspace's own "Dropped!" marks. Section titles show only the drop rule; the "dropped" tag stays on each grade. A note under the total says estimates can be slightly off.

## 1.5.8
Grades: Brightspace's own dropped grades are used instead of working them out, and each "dropped" tag says whether Brightspace or the section's rule dropped it.

## 1.5.7
Grades: in weighted courses, a dropped score's weight goes to the scores that are kept, so drop rules raise the total and What-If estimate as they do in Brightspace.

## 1.5.6
Grades: sections that only have a released total (no individual grades) now show and count. Sections left out of the final grade mark their grades "not counted". Ungraded work is weighted the way the section splits its weight (set by hand, by points, or equally).

## 1.5.5
Ticking off a task no longer reloads the page: the Tasks Panel rings move from where they were, and List View updates just that row.

## 1.5.4
Confetti now lasts 5 seconds.

## 1.5.3
Fixed the Grades page not showing. Search: shorter labels for each kind of result, and long labels are cut off instead of overlapping.

## 1.5.2
Confetti: side cannons in your courses' colours. Help lists search under keyboard shortcuts only.

## 1.5.1
Confetti looks like real confetti (drawn with canvas-confetti, ISC licence).

## 1.5.0
Search across all your courses: from the Dashboard button or by pressing s. Finds module items, assignments, quizzes, discussions, announcements and imported Ed/Gradescope work.

## 1.4.9
Confetti when everything in the Tasks Panel's week is done (once a week; off if your system asks for reduced motion).

## 1.4.8
Ed and Gradescope entries can be pinned too.

## 1.4.7
[Ed] sections are named in the plural (Homeworks, Exams, Weeks).

## 1.4.6
[Ed] sections: only "Other" goes first, and sections of no known kind (such as Week) go after Lessons.

## 1.4.5
[Ed] sections are arranged by kind: Other, Exams, Projects, Homework, Lessons. Numbered modules are grouped under one heading (Homework 1, Homework 2 → Homeworks).

## 1.4.4
[Ed] sub-modules follow Ed's order.

## 1.4.3
The [Ed] module follows Ed's order, with Ed's modules as sub-modules. The [Gradescope] module follows Gradescope's order.

## 1.4.2
Pin module items from the menu on their left. Pinned items show in a Pinned module at the top of Home and Modules.

## 1.4.1
Phone fixes: the course menu visibly folds closed, the Dashboard options menu opens clear of the left menu, and the grade total shows above the grades on narrow screens.
